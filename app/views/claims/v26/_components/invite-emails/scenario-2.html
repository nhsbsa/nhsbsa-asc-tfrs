Before you start

Before you click on the link above, please download the Microsoft Authenticator app onto your phone.

The first time you access the service you'll be shown a QR code. You'll need to scan this using the app to set up multi-factor authentication.

To do this:

  • open the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone
  • select Verified IDs
  • scan the QR code

You'll need to use your authenticator app to confirm your identity each time you log in to the service.
