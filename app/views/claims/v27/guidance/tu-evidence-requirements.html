What evidence you need to provide

There are 2 types of evidence you may need to add when creating a new claim as part of the Claim for adult social care learning and development service:

  • evidence of payment
  • evidence of completion

These are necessary to ensure you get the right amount of reimbursement and to protect against fraud. Without this evidence we will not be able to process the claim and you will not get back any money.

Each piece of evidence must be:

  • in PDF, JPG, JPEG or PNG format
  • an original document
  • clear and in focus
  • not cropped (it must show the whole document, not just part of it)
  • not more than 2MB

If you have a document that is not in digital form, you'll need to scan, photograph or screenshot it.

Evidence of payment

You need to show that you have paid for the training.

Acceptable evidence includes:

  • an invoice and separate receipt or bank statement
  • an invoice that is marked as paid and includes the payment date

Whatever evidence you provide must show the:

  • name of the organisation that made the payment
  • name of the organisation that received the payment, and their VAT number (if they’re not exempt)
  • training or qualification paid for
  • amount paid
  • date of payment – any documents showing the payment date must show the same date and this must match the payment date you enter on the claim details
  • number of learners paid for

You can upload up to 10 files if a single document does not show all the necessary information.

If you’re claiming for Oliver McGowan Mandatory Training (OMMT) procured externally

For a single OMMT training session

Your payment evidence must show:

  • session date
  • unit price including VAT
  • total number of learners

For example:

10 June, unit price £200 including VAT, 10 learners

OR

  • session date
  • total cost of training including VAT
  • total number of learners

For example:

10 June, total cost £2,000 including VAT, 10 learners

We need this information on every claim you submit for this training.

For multiple sessions

Your payment evidence must include:

  • date of each training session
  • cost of each session
  • number of learners per session

For example:

Session 1 – 10 June, £1000 including VAT, 5 learners

Session 2 – 11 June, £1500 including VAT, 9 learners

Session 3 – 12 June, £1800 including VAT, 12 learners

If you’re paying in instalments

Some training providers offer payment plans that allow you to pay for staff training in instalments.

If you’re paying in instalments and choose to use the 60/40 funding model, you can submit evidence of payment in 2 ways.

The first is to provide evidence of payment for 60% of the training costs when you submit the 60 part of your claim. You can then provide further evidence for the remaining 40% with the 40 part.

Alternatively you can submit payment evidence for 100% of the training costs with the 60 part of your claim.

Evidence of completion

You also need to show that each learner completed the course or qualification.

Acceptable evidence would be a dated completion certificate from the training provider. This must include the:

  • learner’s name
  • training provider or awarding organisation

Find out more about creating a new claim.

If you need support with evidence
